Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how Real Estate RE Marketing Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”), operating under the MaliHaus brand, handles information collected through the MaliHaus campaign landing page at getoffer.malihaus.com, its property-enquiry form and its chat tools. References to MaliHaus on this page refer to the brand of Real Estate RE Marketing Inc.

Information collected

When you complete an enquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, property address, property type and condition, ownership or occupancy details, selling circumstances and timeframe, price expectations, preferred contact method and callback time, and any notes you choose to share. We also record your responses, an enquiry summary, and your consent with its timestamp and page address.

The page records campaign parameters, referral source, landing-page information and timestamps to help identify where an enquiry originated. Browser storage supports attribution and helps prevent duplicate submissions. Chat tools may process information you enter into a conversation. Please do not submit passwords, bank details, Social Security numbers or other sensitive identifiers through the form or chat.

How information is used

We use enquiry information to assess your property enquiry, respond to questions, contact you about your property, coordinate follow-up and appointments, and operate our enquiry process. Campaign and interaction information helps us understand how visitors reach and use the page and assess campaign performance.

Service providers

This page is hosted through GitHub Pages. The enquiry form and chat tools use HighLevel/LeadConnector. These providers process information needed to host the page or deliver their services. Our enquiry workflow may also use Zapier and REsimpli for automation and lead management. Third-party services handle information under their own privacy policies as well as the arrangements applicable to our use of their services.

Cookies, browser storage and measurement

The page uses browser storage for enquiry attribution, submission state and duplicate-submission protection. Third-party form, chat and measurement services may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings; restricting them may affect page features. With your permission, the OpenAI Ads Measurement Pixel measures landing-page visits and successfully delivered enquiries to help attribute results to our ChatGPT ads. Automatic advanced matching may detect contact information on the page and normalize and securely hash supported identifiers in your browser before sending them to OpenAI. We do not add property details, selling circumstances or enquiry notes to Pixel events. Measurement is disabled until you choose “Allow measurement.” Choose “Decline,” or use “Advertising privacy choices” on the enquiry page to withdraw permission for future measurement. Events request an opt-out from future user-level personalization. Browser settings may also restrict cookies or storage. Read OpenAI’s privacy policy for its handling of information.

Calls and text messages

If you consent through the enquiry form, MaliHaus, a brand of Real Estate RE Marketing Inc., may contact you using the details you supply. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for help at (321) 655-2099. We will not share or sell mobile data to third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Privacy requests and retention

To ask about information associated with your enquiry, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw permission for future marketing contact, email mmali@malihaus.com. Please identify your enquiry without sending sensitive identification documents. Requests are subject to applicable requirements, including any recordkeeping obligations, and may require verification of your identity.

Links and updates

Links to the main MaliHaus website and other services may have separate privacy notices. We may revise this policy as the landing page and its services change. The effective date above identifies this version.
